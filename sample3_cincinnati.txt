Cincinnati is a city in southwestern Ohio.
The Ohio River forms the southern border of the city.
The Bengals play football at Paycor Stadium.
Skyline Chili is famous for Cincinnati-style chili.
The Reds are the oldest professional baseball team.
Ohio became a state in 1803.
The Cincinnati Zoo is one of the oldest zoos in the country.
Fairfield and Hamilton are north of Cincinnati in Butler County.
The University of Cincinnati was founded in 1819.
Many bridges cross the Ohio River into Kentucky.
